# CLAUDE.md

Portfolio websites for a freelance web designer who targets small businesses
(pilates studios, academies, reservation-based shops). The owner is not a developer:
explain in plain Korean, ask one question at a time with 2–3 options.

## Projects

- `/` (root): OVEN 09, a fictional bakery cafe. Rules in `DESIGN.md`, tokens in `tokens.css`.
- `pilates/`: FORM 08, a fictional private pilates studio (multi-page). Rules in `pilates/DESIGN.md`.

Static HTML/CSS/JS only, deployed with GitHub Pages. No build step.

## Use these skills at the right time

- Choosing or changing a visual direction: `website-design:web-design-system`
- Building or redesigning pages: `frontend-design:frontend-design` (taste and layout)
- After a page is built: `website-design:review-page`, then fix what the owner picks
- Writing or polishing Korean copy: `humanize-kit:humanize`

## Rules

- Fictional brands: mark invented prices, phone numbers and addresses as examples.
- Never invent testimonials or reviews.
- Photos are AI-generated by the owner (Gemini). Crop the Gemini mark at the bottom right,
  save web sizes (1600px and 800px webp), and keep one consistent tone per project.
- Check every page at 375px, 768px and 1440px, and with the keyboard only, before pushing.
